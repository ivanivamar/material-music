Pelagic · Concerts · Material
Tour dates for the artists you already listen to.
An On tour section on the Artist page and a Concerts near you page, built from Ticketmaster data refreshed once a day and matched to a home city and radius set in Settings. Every screen is interactive: chips filter, the location pill opens the city and radius picker, Tickets and artist taps show where they lead.

05
Add a show yourself
For shows Ticketmaster doesn't list. Add show sits in the page header on desktop and is a floating button on phone. Pick an artist from your library, then add a date, venue and city. Start time and ticket link are optional. Your shows sort into the same list with an "Added by you" chip; tap the chip to edit or delete. Tickets appears only if you saved a link. Daily updates never touch these shows.
