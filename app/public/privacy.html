Pelagic privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Pelagic is a personal music player made by one person for his own music library. It is not a commercial service and has no server of its own. This page explains what happens to your data when you sign in with Google.

What Pelagic accesses

When you sign in, Pelagic asks Google for access to your Google Drive. It uses that access only to read and write its own Pelagic folder (the music files, covers, the library catalogue, playlists and settings stored there) and to stream or download your music for playback. It also reads your name, email address and profile picture to show which account is signed in.

Where your data goes

  • Your music and library data stay in your own Google Drive.
  • The app keeps a copy of the library catalogue, cover images, settings and your Google access token on your device (browser storage, or app storage on Android), so it can work offline.
  • Pelagic does not send your data to any server run by its developer, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • To look up new releases, the app may send artist names or identifiers to the public MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive services. No account information is sent.

Use of Google user data

Pelagic's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Choose Sign out in the app's account menu to revoke the token and clear the data cached on that device. You can also remove Pelagic's access at any time from your Google Account under Third-party apps & services. Files in your Drive's Pelagic folder are yours and are never deleted by signing out.
