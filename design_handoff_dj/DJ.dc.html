Built from Pelagic's line language: a horizon line that fades at both ends, a glowing dot on it, and arcs rising above it at falling opacity, with a faint reflection below. The large mark sits on stone, contours and grain and carries the PELAGIC DJ label. Under 100px only the dot, two arcs and the horizon remain. Everything that belongs to the DJ is drawn in gold rgb(226,186,96): the mark, the button, the voice level, the current spoken word, session progress, the DJ chip and the panel controls. Album art keeps colouring the rest of the scene.

06 / REDUCED MOTION
Switch MOTION to Reduced at the top to see it. The mark keeps a static ring and a SPEAKING label instead of sonar rings, the voice level freezes to a still shape, the DJ button keeps its glow without breathing, marine snow disappears, contours stay still, and the hand-over from the mark to the first cover is an instant cut. The words of the spoken line still light up in step with the voice, since that's timing, not movement.
