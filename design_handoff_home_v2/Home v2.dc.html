HOME FEELS INSTANT
Greeting, Jump back in, Recently added, Your artists, Smart playlists and Rediscover come straight from the local catalogue and paint at once. Only Made for you and Radio for you wait on computation: their shelves keep their place and height, with mosaic-shaped and ring-shaped shimmer cards and a mono sub (MIXING FROM 3,912 SONGS · TUNING IN). Real cards replace them in place with a 0.6s fade, no reflow.
FEW PLAYS
Fewer Daily Mixes (one per genre cluster with enough plays, here 2) plus Deep cuts; the shelf says MORE AS YOU LISTEN. Rediscover, the mix and the album shelf, is hidden until there is something to rediscover. Radio is seeded from the artists with the most albums, and Your artists sorts by albums with an N ALBUMS sub.
NO SMART PLAYLISTS
Designer's call: a single invite card in place of the row, not a hidden shelf. Smart playlists are easy to miss and the card is one line tall. Dismissed for good once the first smart playlist exists.
