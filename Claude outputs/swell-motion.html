Swell motion

Four ways for the wave to keep flowing. Every one uses a single smooth curve, so nothing looks jagged. Pause to check that each one settles flat.

1Breathing swell

The crests rise and fall roughly in place, like a swell breathing, and wander slowly back and forth instead of marching one way. It's always one smooth curve.

2Wake

Ripples are born at the thumb and roll back toward the start, like the wake behind a boat. They're strongest near the thumb and calm out toward the left.

3Current

A smooth wave that flows back toward the start at a steady pace, while its height swells and eases in slow, long pulses.

4Tide

The wave drifts one way for a few seconds, slows down, and drifts back, like water sloshing gently. It never stops, and it never only goes right.
