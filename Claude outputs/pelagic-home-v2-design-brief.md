## Brief for Claude Design

Paste this into Claude Design together with the handoff (`D:\pelagic\design_handoff_pelagic\` README + `Pelagic.dc.html`), the DJ handoff (`design_handoff_dj`, for the DJ logo/mark) and screenshots of the current Home (desktop + phone), an album page, a playlist page, a smart playlist page, the song ⋯ menu and the phone action sheet.

**Pelagic Home v2.** Pelagic is a personal FLAC music player (PC PWA + Android) for one person's own library. Visual language: deep-sea / pelagic: dark stone backgrounds, contour lines, grain, soft light pools, "marine snow" particles, album-art tinted scenes, uppercase mono meta labels (IBM Plex Mono), General Sans headings. Match the existing handoff tokens, card styles and motion (slow, glowing, calm). No hearts/likes anywhere.

We're redesigning **Home** and adding **auto-generated mixes** and **radio**. Everything is built from the person's own library and play history (no streaming catalogue, no other users).

New Home order, top to bottom: greeting header (unchanged) → **Jump back in** → yearly recap shelf (existing, only Dec 18–Jan 31) → **Made for you** (mixes) → **Radio for you** → Recently added (existing) → **Your artists** → **Smart playlists** → Rediscover (existing album shelf). The old "Recently listened" and "Most played" album shelves are gone.

Needed (desktop 1440×900 and 1180×760, phone 412×915 and 360×740):
1. **Jump back in**: compact tiles at the top of Home (small cover ~56px + name + tiny mono kind label: ALBUM · PLAYLIST · SMART · DAILY MIX · RADIO · ARTIST). Desktop 4 × 2 grid, phone 2 × 3. Hover (desktop): play button on the tile; the tile of what's playing now glows with the Eq mark. The items are the last things played *from*, synced between PC and phone. Show the row with a mix of kinds, and with only 2–3 items (early use).
2. **DJ tile**: always the first tile, using the existing DJ mark (from the DJ handoff). States: **idle** ("DJ" · "Endless · tap to start"), **on air** (DJ is running: "On air" + current session title, e.g. "Never played · 2010s post-punk", calm alive glow / one slow ring), **intro speaking** (one-ring ripple like the mini-player), hover, pressed. Tap opens the full-screen player in its DJ start state (or into the run); long-press / right-click opens the DJ panel.
3. **Mix cards** ("Made for you" shelf): **Daily Mix 1–6** (each around 1–3 related genres, title like "Darkwave · Post-punk", subtitle "Molchat Doma, Interpol and more"), **Deep cuts** (never-played songs from artists you play a lot) and **Rediscover** (songs you loved but haven't played lately). Need a **generated cover system** that works without designers per mix: e.g. a 2 × 2 mosaic of artist/album art with a tint from the mix's genre, a "DAILY MIX" mark and number, and distinct-but-related looks for Deep cuts and Rediscover. Must look good with very dark and very bright album art, and at 168px (desktop) / 140px (phone) and as a 56px Jump back in tile. Hover: play + shuffle.
4. **Radio cards** ("Radio for you"): stations seeded from an artist (round artist photo) or a song (album cover) with a **RADIO ring / mark** that reads as "this keeps going" (sonar fits the theme, but keep it distinct from the DJ's ripple). Title "Interpol Radio" / "Radio from 'Evil'", subtitle "With Editors, The National…". 
5. **Your artists**: round avatars (desktop ~132px, phone ~104px), name below, mono sub (e.g. "48 PLAYS · 90 DAYS"), hover play; artist with no photo (initials / tinted placeholder).
6. **Smart playlists shelf**: playlist cards (existing mosaic) with "N SONGS · UPDATES ITSELF", ending in a **"New smart playlist"** card (✦ icon).
7. **Mix page** (`#/mix/…`, based on the existing playlist page, read-only): header label `DAILY MIX · MADE FOR YOU · REFRESHES DAILY`, title, a one-line "why" ("Your most played genres lately, with 9 songs you haven't heard yet"), counts/length, Play · Shuffle · **Save as playlist** (+ toast "Saved as 'Darkwave · Post-punk · 19 Oct'" with Open). Phone variant. States: phone offline ("32 OF 50 OFFLINE", missing songs greyed), and an expired link ("This mix has been refreshed" + today's mixes).
8. **Start radio**: the new item in the song, album and artist ⋯ menus (desktop) and the phone action sheet, plus in the full-screen player's ⋯. And how the player context line reads during a radio: "PLAYING FROM RADIO · Interpol".
9. **Loading / empty states**: placeholder cards while mixes and radios are being computed (Home must feel instant); early-history states (few plays: fewer Daily Mixes, Rediscover hidden, Radio seeded from the artists with most albums); no smart playlists (shelf hidden — or a single invite card, designer's call).
10. Overall **Home rhythm**: show the full desktop and phone Home scrolled top to bottom with all shelves, so spacing/hierarchy between the compact tiles, square cards and round avatars reads well.

Reduced motion must still work (no rings or pulses, instant transitions).
