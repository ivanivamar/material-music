Seek bar options

Five replacements for the jagged waveform on the full-screen player, all live. Drag or click any of them to seek, hover to see the hover state, and pause to see how each one rests. The swatches change the album-tint accent.

Accent
AWaterline

The plainest one. A 2px rounded line, played part in the accent with a soft glow. The knob is a small dot that grows and the line thickens to 4px on hover or drag. It matches the slider already used on the bar and the lyrics view.

BLight trail

A 1px hairline. The played part fades in from nothing toward a bright point, like light moving through water, with a small glow pool around the playhead. Very quiet, and it still has a "Pelagic" glow.

CSwell

The played part is a slow, smooth sine wave that drifts while music plays; the part ahead is a flat line. When you pause, the wave settles flat. It keeps the idea of a wave, but it's calm and even instead of jagged.

DTide bars

A row of evenly spaced, rounded bars, all the same low height. The bars that have played light up in the accent. The bar under the playhead stands taller, and its neighbours rise a little with it. Rhythmic but tidy.

EDepth gauge

A hairline with a small tick for every minute of the track, like an instrument dial. The playhead is a short glowing vertical needle. On hover, a mono timestamp appears over the spot under the pointer. Good if you like to know where you are in long tracks.
